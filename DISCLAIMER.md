# Not financial advice

Tickers is a research and education study of equity-forecast models.
It is not investment, tax, or legal advice. It is not a broker, not a
solicitation, and not an offer of securities.

Forecasts and the published error tables can be wrong. A past error is not
a future error and not a future return. Do not use this study as the sole
basis for any trade or financial decision.

Walk-forward training error (MAE, RMSE, MAPE, R²) is not the same number as
a later week-ahead result. A negative R² means the model did worse than
predicting the training-window average. A large one-day price move with a
negative R² is not a tight call.

Prices downloaded at runtime come from third parties through yfinance.
They are not an official exchange feed.
